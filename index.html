Progress & study rewards

Tasks earn 10 XP (up to 5 task rewards a day), assignments 20 XP, a manually completed 5-minute focus block 15 XP, and each daily quest 20 XP once. Achievements award 10 XP once. Levels use fixed personal XP thresholds. Streaks count distinct local dates with genuine completed study actions. Your data stays in this browser.
